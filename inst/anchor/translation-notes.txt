The original English words are machine translated into other langagues using LLM
(Sonnet 5 via Posit Assistant). For example:

Translate English words in inst/anchor/en.yml to Simplified Chinese and save as inst/anchor/zh_cn.yml.
Use the original words as the keys in the new YAML file. Do not read or update other files.

Translate English words in inst/anchor/en.yml to Persian and save as inst/anchor/fa.yml.
Use the original words as the keys in the new YAML file.
